## English

**Title:** English Games – Learn & Speak
**Short:** Shoot words and shadow YouTube videos to practise speaking English.

**Full:** Two games for kids (Cambridge Flyers) and adults. Word Shooter: type the falling word to shoot it, with built-in sets or your own. Shadowing: paste a YouTube link and practise sentence by sentence — listen, repeat, speak along. No transcript? Tap ✂ at the end of each sentence and the app replays it, then it is your turn. Paste a transcript or read one from screenshots, entirely on your device. Adjustable speed and repeats, progress per lesson. No account, no ads, data stays on your device.
